Inference-health Pareto diagnostic
---------------------------------
The empirical-Bayes generalized Pareto fit and tail-selection convention in
ParetoTail.scala are adapted from ArviZ v0.22.0, arviz/stats/stats.py
(_gpdfit and _psislw), copyright ArviZ developers, Apache License 2.0.
https://github.com/arviz-devs/arviz/tree/v0.22.0
See ArviZ-LICENSE.txt (also included in binary/source/API artifacts).
Changes: Scala diagnostic-only port, stable candidate-weight normalization,
bounded input, interruption checks and explicit unavailable-result handling.
The unchanged upstream _gpdfit function is retained in the research-only
tools/inference_health_reference.py for deterministic reference checks.
No ArviZ runtime dependency or PSIS weight smoothing is included.
